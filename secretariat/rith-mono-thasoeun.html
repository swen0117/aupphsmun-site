Welcome letter

Thasoeun Rith Mono

Hello everyone,

I am Rith Mono Thasoeun, and I will be your Head of Admin for this year AUPPHS-FAMUN, with this incredible time of year to be your host welcoming you all to this amazing conference, I am grateful to be part of the team working behind the scenes. The Administration Department works to ensure that all operational aspects of the conference run smoothly, creating an environment where delegates can focus on meaningful debates, collaboration, and diplomacy.

On behalf of the organizing team, I warmly welcome you to our conference. Beyond the resolution and speeches, I hope you also take the time to enjoy the experience and create memories that last longer after the final gavel. So enjoy the debates, enjoy the chaos, enjoy the journey, and make this conference worth remembering.

Thank you, and remember, you only live once.
