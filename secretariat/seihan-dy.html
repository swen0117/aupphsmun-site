Welcome letter

Dy Seihan

Hello everyone,

I am Dy Seihan, and I am thrilled to serve as your Deputy Head of Design for this year's AUPPHS-FAMUN. It is an incredible privilege to work alongside our Head of Media and the rest of the Secretariat to shape the visual identity and storytelling of this conference. Behind the scenes, our media team is dedicated to capturing the energy, dedication, and memorable moments that make this event so special.

On behalf of the organizing team, I extend a warm welcome to AUPPHS-FAMUN 2026. I am excited to witness the incredible debates, diplomacy, and connections that will unfold over the coming days. I wish you all an inspiring, successful, and truly unforgettable experience!
