AUPPHS-FA III GA1 Chairs

01 · Head Chair

Mok Channatalyn

My name is Mok Channatalyn, Natalyn for short, and I am proud to serve as your Head Chair for the first general assembly at AUPPHS-FAMUN III. To get to know me a little: I'm 16 years old, I love to read and write, and almost everything I own is yellow! As for MUN, I have been an avid participant for the past 2 years and I've learnt many valuable lessons from my fellow delegates and previous chairs. For that reason, I hope to encourage a diplomatic and cooperative environment where all delegates can learn from one another and leave the conference as future change makers.

Best of luck and see you guys soon!

If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to me (mokchannatalyn@gmail.com) or your Deputy Chairs.

02 · Deputy Chair

Ung Phechseang

Hello fellow delegates!

My name is Phechseang Ung, and I am honored to serve as one of your Deputy Chairs for DISEC here at AUPPHS-FA MUN III.

This will be my 7th conference, and whether it is your first or your tenth conference, don't be shy, don't be scared. I want to make our committee into a space where you feel comfortable speaking up, asking questions, and challenging yourself by getting out of your comfort zones. This conference is a chance for us to connect, grow, and challenge ourselves through debate and diplomacy.

I look forward to meeting each of you and witnessing the thoughtful debates and innovative solutions you will bring to committee. Wishing everyone the very best in preparation, and I'll see you at AUPPHS-FA MUN III!

Chair Report
03 · Second Deputy Chair

Chenn Sharita

Greetings, delegates!

My name is Sharita, currently a junior at CIA FIRST International School. I'm truly honored to be serving you as your 2nd deputy chair for DISEC at this year’s AUPPHS-FA MUN III.
