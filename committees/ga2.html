Chair team

AUPPHS-FA III GA2 Chairs

01 · Head Chair

Koeut Sovanreach

Greetings delegates!

My name is Reach, an 11th grader attending LFRD. It is my utmost pleasure and honor to serve as your Head Chair in this year’s AUPPMUN conference.

Whether you are new to MUN or an experienced delegate, do not be afraid to speak, make mistakes, and learn from one another. With that said, I encourage all of you to challenge yourselves, defend your stance with confidence, and engage fully.

Alongside my fellow Deputy Chairs, we will ensure that this conference is memorable and enjoyable for all.

I look forward to meeting all of you, hearing your insightful debates, and your approaches to pressing global issues.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any inquiries or concerns.

See you all soon!
