01 · Head Chair

Khoo Xiang Hui

My name is Xiang Hui (Melody), a sophomore at ISPP, and it is my utmost pleasure and honour to serve as your head chair for JGA1 at AUPPHS-FA MUN III. I will do my best to ensure that this conference will go smoothly and be filled with fruitful debates and unforgettable memories! I understand how nerve-wracking this experience may be (or not), but either way, my fellow chairs and I will do our utmost best to support and guide you. Please do not hesitate to reach out to me at @29khoox.ispp.edu.kh.

Chair Report 29khoox.ispp.edu.kh
02 · Deputy Chair

Ean Chealini

My name is Chealini Ean (Lini), and it is my distinct honor to serve as the Deputy Chair of JGA1 of the AUPPHS-FA MUN III. By the time of this MUN, I will be a sophomore at CIA First International School. I look forward to working alongside with the head chair, 2nd deputy chair and all of you delegates to make JGA1 a place where insightful debates are made, problems are solved creatively, and above all mutual understanding is established within the committee.

This MUN marks my 8th experience, and through my experience, I want to remind all delegates that every voice in the room matters; whether you speak 100 times or once, your ideas have the power to shape the discussion and the committee's outcome. So don't be afraid to ask questions and step out of your comfort zone, because that is where true growth is established.

Chair Report
03 · Second Deputy Chair

Ung Liyan

My name is Liyan, a sophomore from AUPPHS-FA. It is my absolute pleasure to serve as your Head Chair for this year’s MUN conference.

As this is my first time serving as deputy Chair, I am truly excited to meet you all and experience the unique ideas and diplomacy you bring to the committee. I hope this conference will create lasting memories, friendships, and gain valuable skills and experiences.

I look forward to seeing you all in committee so please don't hesitate to contact me or the deputy chair if you have any concerns. Good luck and have fun!
