Chair team

AUPPHS-FA III ICJ Chairs

01 · Head Chair

Kry Darayuth

Hello Delegates,

My name is Darayuth Kry, a Grade 11 student at CIA First International School, Chbar Ampov. It is my honor to serve as the President of the International Court of Justice (ICJ) for AUPPMUN 2026. This conference marks my 21st conference and my 9th time chairing.

As ICJ follows procedures that differ from traditional MUN committees, I do not expect perfection. Instead, effort, preparation, and a willingness to engage in the process.

Whether you are serving as a Judge, Representative, or Witness, I hope our debates will be engaging and productive as we work together to uncover the truth of the matters before the Court and bring justice to those who deserve it.

I am looking forward to an engaging and fruitful debate. Please feel free to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.

See you soon, delegates! :D

Chair Report
02 · Deputy Chair

Samedy Puthineath

Greetings, Delegates!

My name is Puthineath Samedy, a junior from LFRD, and I am honored to serve as your Vice President for the International Court of Justice (ICJ) at AUPPHS-FA MUN III. Having participated in around five MUN conferences as a delegate, I am excited to take on the role of chair for the first time. While this is a new experience for me, I am committed to creating a welcoming environment where every delegate feels comfortable contributing. If you have any questions before the conference, please don't hesitate to reach out. I look forward to meeting all of you, see you soon!

03 · Second Deputy Chair

Cheamuny Virak

Greetings, fellow delegates.

My name is Virak Cheamuny, and I am grade 10 at CIA First. And this will be the first time I will be chairing for the ICJ and actually chairing in general for MUNs. And I must admit I am very excited yet quite nervous. But as your vice president for this conference, I hope that I can make ICJ a fun, engaging committee, as, during my time in ICJ, I have grown very fond of it, and I hope that everyone will truly enjoy ICJ, like I did. And finally, I would wish you all delegates the best of luck and hope that this conference is a success.

And please do not hesitate to reach out to me if you have any enquiries!
