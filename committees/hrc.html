Hello everyone! I’m your Head Chair, Sophie, and it’s a pleasure to meet you all very soon. This will be my sixth and last time chairing in Cambodia.

Chair Report
02 · Deputy Chair

San Channimol

Hello delegates!

My name is Channimol, and I am honored to serve as one of your Chairs for the Human Rights Council at AUPPHS-FA MUN III. Through my countless experience in Model United Nations, as a delegate I have developed a strong understanding of diplomacy, debate, and committee procedures. These experiences have taught me the importance of fairness, professionalism, and respectful communication.

As your Chair, my goal is to create a welcoming and organized committee where every delegate feels comfortable sharing their ideas. Whether you are attending your first MUN or have participated in many conferences, I encourage you to speak confidently, think critically, and work together to find meaningful solutions. Remember that MUN is not only about winning awards but also about learning, improving your skills, and respecting different perspectives.

I hope our committee will be filled with productive debate, creative resolutions, and an atmosphere where everyone can enjoy the experience while growing as delegates. I look forward to meeting all of you and wish you the best of luck with your preparation. See you in the committee!
